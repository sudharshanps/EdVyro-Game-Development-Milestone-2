Firewall Shift

Protect the gateway by matching your firewall mode to each incoming packet.

● SAFE → ALLOW    ◆ THREAT → BLOCK
Switch with Space or tap. Get 30 correct and survive 3 minutes.
How to play

Match the packet

Each labeled packet travels toward the gateway. Use a single action to switch between ALLOW and BLOCK before it reaches the dashed line.

✓SAFE packet → use ALLOW
✕THREAT packet → use BLOCK

Press SPACE or tap the game area

Mission control

Keep the gateway online

Correct match: +10 points. Wrong match: lose one life. Reach at least 30 correct matches, with a life remaining when the clock reaches zero, to win.
